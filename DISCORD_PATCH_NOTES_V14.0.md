# 🏁 FDH v14.0 — League Experience Update

FDH v14.0 is now ready, with a major upgrade to league pages and featured content.

## 🏆 Completed Races
- Every league page now has a **Completed Races** section.
- Scheduled races automatically move into the archive **90 minutes after their start time**.
- Races still marked as live will remain out of the archive until live status is ended.
- Completed cards include the event, circuit, local date/time, artwork, and replay/event links when available.

## 🌐 FDH Drivers Website
- Added a new **FDH Drivers Website ↗** button.
- Available from the homepage and every league page.
- Links directly to the official FDH drivers website.

## 🎨 Badge Redesign
- **League of the Week** and **Race of the Week** badges now use a darker FDH-themed design.
- **Featured** badges have also been updated to better match the black-and-red website style.
- Improved borders, shadows, spacing, and hover consistency.

## 📱 Additional Polish
- Completed race cards adapt cleanly across desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- League archives are sorted from newest to oldest.

🔗 **FDH Drivers Website:** https://fp-league.github.io/FDHSITE/
